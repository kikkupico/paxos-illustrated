# Cover Prompts — Volumes I–IX

Work order for redrawing the nine volume covers so they share **one design** — frame, masthead, title and
byline lettering — while each **picture** is its own.

- **Gold standard:** the Volume V cover the site actually shows — `images/cover.png` (“The Part-Time
  Parliament”, used by `index.html` and `volume-5.html`). Its frame, masthead box, title lettering and
  byline are the design template, and its calm “a world and its people” approach is the model for the
  pictures. Its *picture* (hilltop temple, sunny bay, ships, walkers on a path) is **not** a template. `images/cover_volume_5.png` (“The Synod of the Part-Time
  Parliament”) is **not** used by any page and is not the reference.
- **The one change to the template:** the masthead logo. The plain laurel wreath becomes a **laurel wreath
  crown with a “P” inside it**, and the volume number in **Roman numerals** goes directly **below the logo**,
  above the existing “PAXOS COMICS” label.
- **Content:** each cover shows the volume's setting and the kind of people who live in it (an army for
  Volume II, monks for Volume IX, and so on). It does not depict any mechanism from the paper — no votes,
  messages, counters, ledgers of numbered lines or diagrams — and it does not need to match any named
  character from the chronicle.
- **Variety:** the design never changes; the picture always does. No two covers share a viewpoint, time of
  day, weather, landscape or palette, and no stock elements are reused (not every cover gets a coastline, a
  sunset, ships or a hilltop temple). See the table in §2.

Run these as **batch 1** of `IMAGE_MASTER_PROMPT.md`, before any panel is generated.

## 1. Files and order

| Vol | Output (then `tools/compress.py`) | Shown by | Reference image(s) to attach |
|---|---|---|---|
| V | `images/cover.png` → `cover_small.webp` | `index.html`, `volume-5.html` | the current `images/cover.png` (edit it) |
| I–IV, VI–IX | `images/cover_volume_N.png` → `cover_volume_N_small.webp` | `index.html` | the **new** `images/cover.png` |

1. **Volume V first.** It is an edit of the existing cover (§3). Review it: the logo, numeral and label must
   be clean and the rest of the cover unchanged. Every other cover copies its masthead, so do not continue
   until it is right.
2. **Then the other eight**, each with the new Volume V cover attached as the layout reference and the
   prompt from §4. The reference is for the frame, masthead and lettering **only**;
   the picture must come from the prompt. Regenerate any cover that borrows the reference's picture (a
   hilltop temple, a sunny bay, striped sails, figures walking up a winding path) or repeats another cover's
   look from the table in §2.
3. All covers are **2:3 portrait** (the existing files are 848×1264). No HTML changes are needed: the pages
   already point at these filenames.

For each cover, use the built-in image generation tool: give it the prompt exactly as written (every prompt
is self-contained), attach the reference image from the table, ask for 2:3 portrait, and save the result
over the output file as a PNG. Then make the web copy:

```bash
python3 tools/compress.py cover            # Volume V → images/cover_small.webp
python3 tools/compress.py cover_volume_3   # others → images/cover_volume_N_small.webp
```

Never crop a cover to fix its shape — that cuts into the masthead or title. Regenerate instead.

The old covers are committed, so `git checkout -- images/cover.png` restores one if an edit goes wrong.

## 2. Shared template

Every prompt in §4 is assembled from these blocks, so the covers match. Edit here first if the template
has to change, then keep §4 in step.

**Style.**
> Vintage 1970s comic book cover illustration: bold black ink outlines, visible halftone dot shading, slightly aged newsprint paper texture with softly worn cream edges, flat vibrant palette (aegean blue #136f9e, terracotta red #bf4a26, olive green #66722c, marble white, sand #f2e7cd, gold accents #c1912b, ink #1c1512). Clean, readable, heroic composition. Ancient/classical-era clothing and objects only; no modern objects.

**Design** (placeholders in braces are filled per volume).
> COVER DESIGN (portrait 2:3). Copy the attached reference cover EXACTLY for the design elements — frame, masthead box, title lettering and byline lettering — and NOTHING ELSE: do not reuse its picture, composition, landscape, sea, sky, colours of the scene or figures. FRAME: a cream paper margin with softly worn edges and a thin black rule framing the whole artwork. MASTHEAD BOX in the top-left corner: a tall rectangle about one-sixth of the cover wide and one-quarter of the cover tall, butting against the top and left of the frame, with a black outline and a flat peach-orange fill. Inside it, from top to bottom: (1) THE LOGO — an olive-green laurel wreath crown (two leafy laurel branches rising from a tied base and curving into an almost-closed circle, open at the top) with a bold black capital letter "P" in a classical serif face centred inside the wreath, the P filling about half the wreath's inner height; (2) directly below the wreath, the Roman numeral "{numeral}" in large bold black classical serif capitals, centred in the box; (3) at the bottom of the box, a white square label with a black outline reading "PAXOS" over "COMICS" in black hand-lettered capitals. TITLE to the right of the masthead box, across the top of the cover: huge cream-white block capitals with Greek-inspired angular letterforms (A like a lambda, E like a sigma), thick black outline and a grey-blue drop shadow, reading {title}. BYLINE right-aligned just under the title: a small italic lowercase "by" followed by "{byline}" in black hand-lettered capitals (if the art behind it is dark, give the letters a thin cream outline). ARTWORK fills the rest of the frame edge to edge. Behind the title keep a calm, low-detail area of the scene (sky, mist, canvas, rock face or shadow) so the lettering stays readable. The viewpoint, composition, time of day, weather, landscape and palette come ONLY from the SCENE below.

### Variety across the set

The design is identical on all nine; the pictures must not repeat. Each prompt in §4 pins down a distinct
look:

| Vol | Viewpoint and setting · light and weather · palette |
|---|---|
| I | High view down a steep village lane · dawn fog · pink-lavender and whitewash |
| II | Inside the camp at night · torchlight and campfires · deep blue and fire orange |
| III | Low view up the sacred way to towering cliffs · hazy midday · blue-grey stone and gold |
| IV | On deck at sea, tilted horizon · storm breaking into calm · slate grey to aegean blue |
| V | *(existing)* Hilltop temple above a sunny bay, figures on a winding path · clear day · aegean blue and olive |
| VI | Interior, looking along a colonnade into storerooms · lamplight and shafts of sun · terracotta and ochre |
| VII | Worm's-eye view up at the gate · grey, rain-washed morning · iron grey, wet stone and bronze |
| VIII | Down in the quarry pit · harsh noon sun and dust · white marble, brick red and sand |
| IX | Forest clearing at blue hour · mist and candlelight · dark pine green, grey and warm gold |

Volume IV is the only cover at sea and Volume V the only one on a coast; every other prompt rules out
coastlines and sunsets explicitly.

**Scene rule.**
> The scene is an establishing view of this volume's world and its people — not an illustration of any rule, procedure, vote, message exchange or diagram. No one is counting, voting, signing or passing tokens; no arrows, charts or numbered objects.

**Text rule.**
> TEXT RULE: the only lettering anywhere on the cover is the P in the logo, the Roman numeral, PAXOS COMICS, the title and the byline, each spelled exactly as given. No other writing, numbers, labels, signs, inscriptions or speech balloons anywhere in the artwork; scrolls, tablets and banners are blank or carry only abstract marks.

### Checklist for every cover

- Masthead box top-left: wreath crown with a **P** inside → Roman numeral below it → white **PAXOS COMICS**
  label. Same box size, fill and outline on all nine.
- Numeral correct: I, II, III, IV, V, VI, VII, VIII, IX.
- Title and byline spelled exactly as given; no extra words, and no stray lettering anywhere in the art.
- Title sits on a calm, low-detail part of the picture; the art fills the frame; cream worn margin and thin
  black rule around it.
- The picture matches its row in the variety table and does not echo the Volume V picture or any other cover.
- The scene reads as *a place and its people*, not as a step of an algorithm.
- It sits comfortably beside the new Volume V cover in the 3×3 grid on `index.html`.
